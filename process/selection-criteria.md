# Selection criteria

The archive prioritizes exceptional tennis dresses made for a particular professional player, including documented athlete collaborations. A championship win alone is not a reason to include a conventional kit.

Look for an unmistakable silhouette, unusual construction, substantial embellishment or a specific personal design story. Prefer the actual competition garment. An elaborate entrance jacket over an ordinary dress does not qualify as a separate featured look.

Verify the player, event, date, design attribution and exact photographed version before reconstructing it. Distinguish bespoke pieces from athlete-designed collections sold at retail; never claim exclusivity without evidence. Retail availability alone does not erase a remarkable design, but ordinary mass-market kits are outside the selection.

Keep front and back renders consistent in background, scale and positioning. Check the rear silhouette against rear-render-guidelines.md and disclose inferred construction.

Removed at the curator's request: Evolving ceremony, The tennis-ball jacket, New York rays, The first title kit, and Peach, then a title.
